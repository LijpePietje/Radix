# Privacy Policy for Radix App

*Last updated: November 22, 2026*

## 1. Data on your device

The app uses the following data on your device only; it is not sent to us or to anyone else:

- Birth data you enter
- Your location (only when permission is given)

The only data that leaves your device are the error reports in section 4.

## 2. Use of data

The collected data is only used for:

- Calculating horoscopes
- Saving your personal settings
- Showing your current location (if allowed)

## 3. Data storage

- Your horoscopes, places and settings stay on your device only
- Apart from the error reports in section 4, no data is sent to external servers
- You can delete your data at any time by clearing the app data

## 4. Error reports

If something goes wrong, the app sends a technical report to Firebase Crashlytics (Google). The report says what went
wrong and where in the app, not whose it is and not with which horoscope. It also contains the make and model of your
device, the Android version, the app version and a random number per installation. No names, birth data or places are
deliberately sent. If the app crashes in a way it cannot catch itself, the error message may by chance contain a name or
place.

You can turn this off under About this app (Send error reports); nothing is sent after that.

## 5. Permissions

The app asks for the following permissions:

- Location access (optional): to determine your current location

## 6. Third parties

This app uses:

- Swiss Ephemeris for astronomical calculations
- Google Play Services for location
- Firebase Crashlytics (Google), only for error reports

## 7. Updates to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated. Please check regularly for updates.
